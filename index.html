The problem

A zoo visit is memorable, but it is rarely interactive.

Passive visits

Younger visitors are used to fast, interactive apps. A normal zoo visit is "look and walk", and the information boards are easy to skip.


One and done

Many people treat the zoo as a checklist, look at the animal and leave without learning anything new, so there is little reason to return.


No live visitor data

Zoos manage large spaces and big crowds without real-time data, so they can't tell which exhibits hold attention or where people bunch up.


Hard to place staff

Without that data it is difficult to put staff where they are needed or to steer crowds away from the busiest areas.

Our aim

Turn a zoo visit into something people want to repeat.

Boost engagement

Scanning a QR code at an exhibit brings up facts, quests and points, so visitors explore actively instead of just looking.

Increase retention

Points, quests and events turn the visit into a game and give people a reason to come back.

Raise lifetime value

Rewards, challenges and quizzes keep visitors exploring more exhibits and spending more time at the zoo.

Bite-sized learning

Short audio guides and fun facts let visitors learn passively while they watch the animals.

Data for the zoo

QR scans, exhibit visits and route choices feed dashboards that help management understand visitor movement and plan staffing.
